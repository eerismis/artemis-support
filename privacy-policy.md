# Privacy Policy for Artemis

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Artemis ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of the Artemis mobile application.

## 1. Personal Information
We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal identification information to others. Artemis is designed to secure your financial data and credentials.

## 2. Authentication
Artemis utilizes "Sign in with Apple" to provide a secure authentication mechanism. We only receive the minimal user identifiers provided by Apple to establish your account safely.

## 3. Data Storage
All credit, card, and payment schedule data you enter into the application is processed securely. We implement industry-standard data security practices to protect your information against unauthorized access.

## 4. AI Processing
Document and invoice processing via our AI module is performed strictly for extracting schedule parameters and is not used for external data profiling.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official support channel.
